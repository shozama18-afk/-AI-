1/**
2 * Copyright (c) 2023-2026 Air Systems Research Center, Acquisition, Technology & Logistics Agency(ATLA)
3 * @file
4 * @brief　文献[Ekker 1994]に示されている誘導弾空力モデルを、[Pitts 1957](NACA-TR-1307)に基づいてテーパ翼と前翼での制御に対応させた誘導弾モデル。
5 *
6 * @details
7 * Based on [Ekker 1994] Appendix. B, but modified a bit.
8 * (1) All values should be provided in MKS units. (angles should be in rad.)
9 * (2) Transonic region (M=0.95 to 1.2) is also calculated by same method as either sub/supersonic region to simplify, although it is not so accuarate.
10 * (3) Extended to tapered planform and canard-controlled missiles based on [Pitts 1957](NACA-TR-1307)
11 * (4) Induced drag on tail is calculated with the effect of downwash, which [Ekker 1994] seems to be ignore by dε/dα≒1.
12 *
13 * References:
14 * [Ekker 1994] Ekker, David A. "Missile Design Toolbox." Diss. Monterey, California. Naval Postgraduate School, 1994.
15 * [Pitts 1957] Pitts, William C., et al. "Lift and Center of Pressure of Wing-Body-Tail Combinations at Subsonic, Transonic, and Supersonic Speeds." NACA-TR-1307, 1957.
16 */
