Detailed Description

　文献[Ekker 1994]に示されている誘導弾空力モデルを、[Pitts 1957](NACA-TR-1307)に基づいてテーパ翼と前翼での制御に対応させた誘導弾モデル。

Copyright (c) 2023-2026 Air Systems Research Center, Acquisition, Technology & Logistics Agency(ATLA)

Based on [Ekker 1994] Appendix. B, but modified a bit. (1) All values should be provided in MKS units. (angles should be in rad.) (2) Transonic region (M=0.95 to 1.2) is also calculated by same method as either sub/supersonic region to simplify, although it is not so accuarate. (3) Extended to tapered planform and canard-controlled missiles based on [Pitts 1957](NACA-TR-1307) (4) Induced drag on tail is calculated with the effect of downwash, which [Ekker 1994] seems to be ignore by dε/dα≒1.

References: [Ekker 1994] Ekker, David A. "Missile Design Toolbox." Diss. Monterey, California. Naval Postgraduate School, 1994. [Pitts 1957] Pitts, William C., et al. "Lift and Center of Pressure of Wing-Body-Tail Combinations at Subsonic, Transonic, and Supersonic Speeds." NACA-TR-1307, 1957.
